My Interest - One Piece

My Interest My Hero

One Piece is a manga and anime series by author Eiichiro Oda, beginning in 1997. It is mostly an action adventure story and I like it for that reason. And also because of the character and setting designs, the world building and the story.

The action is quite over the top and often unconventional making it very fun to watch. It also makes for a lot of funny moments throughout the series. Because of how dense the One Piece world is, every new reveal gets more and more exciting and I'm always in awe after every new chapter.
