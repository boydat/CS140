These are places I've visited and lived in during my time in New Zealand. They are all in different parts of Auckland, the largest city in the country. Manuwera is the most ghetto area, while Howick is more family-friendly. The others are somewhat in the inbetween but lean more on the safe side. These ares spread across Auckland in all directions except out West. Each location has it's own vibe and once you've lived there a while you can feel it easier but despite their differences, they all have a local dairy to get a meat pie from.
