Pokémon Go Club Website

  • Site title and topic

    Pokémon Go Club Website — a website for a potential BYU-Hawaii Pokémon Go club.

  • Why I chose this topic

    Pokémon Go is built to foster friendship and all sorts of social connections to a degree. Laie is a great place to play Pokémon go for various game mechanic reasons and also because it has such a condensed unit of people. I want to make a website for a potential BYU-Hawaii Pokémon Go club to promote it.

  • Home page and five pages

    • Home — welcome, quick links in nav, about the club.
    • Activities — table and schedule of club events and gathers lined up with and without official Pokémon go events.
    • Beginners — tips for beginners in laie specifically and how to level up and have fun quickly. Player interactions like trading or raid planning.
    • Club members — gallery of events, about the presidency.
    • Sign up — sign up sheet.
    • Contact — contact info.

  • Form fields and table information

    The form will collect Full Name, email, pokemon username, trainer code, and current trainer level.

    The activities table will include the event, date and time, and location.
