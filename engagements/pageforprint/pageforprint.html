About the Incredibles

  • They are a family of two parents and three children, Bob and Helen are the parents and Violet, Dash and Jack-jack are the children.
  • Each of them hold super powers and live in a time where supers are shunned and rejected by society, requiring them to blend in.
  • They are a fictional family from the movie series, "The Incredibles".

Suited Up

Bob Parr

Bob is the father of the family and has super strength. He is formally Mister Incredible, one of the top heroes of the golden age. He struggles with the mundane office job life and seeks hero action secretly.

Helen Parr

Helen is the mother and is super flexible and stretchy. She is formerly Elastagirl, another strong hero of the golden age. She is incredibly capable, a great leader and quick-witted.

Violet Parr

Violet is the eldest child of the Parr family in her teens. She starts off insecure and shy in the beginning of the movie but later becomes more confident. She is able to create force fields and turn invisible.
