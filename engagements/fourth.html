What are special entity characters?

Some characters are reserved in HTML. If you use the less than (<) or greater than (>) signs in your HTML text, the browser might mix them with tags. Entity names or entity numbers can be used to display reserved HTML characters.

What are phrase tags?

HTML phrase tags are unique purpose tags designed to use indefinite cases, even though they're implemented in the same manner as other tags are used, which you might have seen in previous chapters. What is the difference between OL and UL Tags?

Ordered or unordered. OL is numbered list while UL is bullet points. In ordered lists the order of the items is important.
