My Hero - Monkey D. Luffy

My Interests My Hero

Luffy is the main character of the One Piece series. I love him for his goal. In name, it is to become king of the pirates. It means many different things, like being the strongest in the world or owning the most treasure or to have the most power over others, but to him, it's to be the most free.

Affiliation:
Captain of the Straw Hat Pirates, Straw Hat Grand Fleet, and Emperor of the Sea.
Powers:
Wielder of the Gomu Gomu no Mi (Rubber-Rubber Fruit) and uses his rubber body to attack and makes use of rubbers properties for defense.
Hometown:
Luffy grew up in Windmill Village on Dawn Island partially with his Grandpa, Garp, who later gave him and his older brothers to the mountain bandits to be raised.
